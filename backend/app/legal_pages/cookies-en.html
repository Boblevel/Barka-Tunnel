Android application

Language, theme, settings and some operational states are stored locally. These technical records are not advertising cookies.

Web panels

Panels use browser session storage for authentication and navigation choices. Clearing this storage or signing out may end the session. Do not share a browser signed in to a panel.

External services and controls

Payment pages and external links may use their own cookies and storage. Read their policies before use. Manage cookies in your browser or clear local Android data; this may remove saved preferences and access details. Contact RHAFF SERVICE at barkatunnel@gmail.com.

Managing data on your phone

Barka Tunnel settings let you change the language and theme. Android settings let you clear local app data; this may remove stored profiles and states and require a new synchronization. This action is separate from a request to delete data held by the server.
